# Figure roles

`Figure4.tif` and `Figure5.tif` are byte-identical copies of the figures supplied with the original manuscript. They are the only Figure 4 and Figure 5 files in this directory intended to represent the manuscript artwork.

The directory `internal_audit_not_for_submission/` contains post-submission forecast and temporal-precedence sensitivity displays. Those displays document checks performed during revision, but they were not requested as replacement figures, were not used to replace the manuscript figures, and must not be submitted as Figure 4 or Figure 5.

`../manifests/figure4_figure5_roles_and_checksums.csv` records the role, byte count, and SHA-256 checksum of every Figure 4/5 artifact in this package.
