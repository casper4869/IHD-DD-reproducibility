Figure 4. Projected trajectories of countries and territories with co-high ischaemic heart disease and depressive disorder incidence, 1992–2030.
A, VARX; B, ARIMAX. Blue solid lines with circles show historical annual counts for 1992–2021, and red dashed lines with triangles show conditional projections for 2022–2030; the vertical grey dashed line marks the transition to the projection period. Future SDI was held at its 2021 value. The archived trajectories reproduced here end at 19 (VARX) and 14 (ARIMAX) in 2030; re-execution of the retained code reproduced 19 and yielded 13, respectively. Both panels use identical axes. The ±15% visual bands shown in the submitted figure were removed because they were not model-derived confidence or prediction intervals. These model-dependent scenarios should not be interpreted as precise expected country counts.
