# Internal forecast-audit interpretation draft

> **Scope:** Historical post-submission audit/sensitivity draft only. It is not approved manuscript text and its `Figure 4a/4b` references concern the internal audit display, not the revised minimal Figure 4.


## Results

The number of countries simultaneously in the highest annual incidence quartile for IHD and DD was 14 in 1992 and 15 in 2021. Under the original fixed-SDI specifications, the reproducible VARX and ARIMAX scenarios yielded 19 and 13 such countries in 2030, respectively (Figure 4a). However, 176 of 204 fitted VARX models were unstable, and the VARX scenario depended on historical-range clipping. In a common-log-scale sensitivity without that clipping, ARIMAX yielded 15 countries in 2030, whereas non-finite VARX forecasts prevented calculation of a complete-denominator count from 2026 (Figure 4b). These results indicate substantial specification sensitivity and do not support a precise estimate of the number of co-high countries in 2030.

## Abstract option

Exploratory projections were sensitive to model specification; instability in country-level VARX models and unquantified forecast uncertainty precluded a precise 2030 estimate.

## Required reporting correction

The former +/-15% count bands were not statistical confidence intervals and have been removed. Forecasts are conditional on constant future SDI. No joint uncertainty interval for the country count has been validated. The revised minimal Figure 4 preserves the archived ARIMAX trajectory ending at 14 because that value appears in both the submitted artwork and archived classification, while the current raw-scale rerun ending at 13 is disclosed as implementation uncertainty. Neither value should be treated as a precise expected count.
