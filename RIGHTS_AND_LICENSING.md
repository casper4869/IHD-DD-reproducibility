No additional permission to reproduce, modify, redistribute, or create derivative works is granted beyond applicable law and the hosting platforms' terms.

- **Included MR evidence:** the archive contains a historical OpenGWAS catalogue metadata snapshot and author-generated aggregate analytical outputs. Their inclusion does not grant rights in the underlying GWAS datasets. Users must determine and comply with the terms and citation requirements of each original dataset.
- **Materials not redistributed:** where redistribution rights were not established, the repository provides identifiers, retrieval instructions, known metadata, and local fingerprints instead of source files.

The Zenodo record therefore uses `Other (Not Open)` (`other-closed`) as its rights identifier while keeping file access open for transparency and verification. File-specific notices and original provider terms control over this summary. Users are responsible for checking the current terms before retrieving or reusing third-party data.
